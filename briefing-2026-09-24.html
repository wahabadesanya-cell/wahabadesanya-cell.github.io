Ireland 21 September 2026

DPC fines Google €403m over Web & App Activity, Location History, Location Accuracy

The DPC's fourth-largest fine to date covers three Google features processing location data between May 2018 and February 2020, finding failures on lawfulness, fairness, transparency and retention limits — people could be profiled and targeted with ads without ever realising their location was the input. For anyone running location-aware features through an AI pipeline (personalisation, agentic assistants, ad targeting), the practical read is that burying the processing in a privacy policy does not satisfy transparency, and indefinite or undefined retention needs an active, documented justification. Coming weeks after the DPC's HSE decision, this confirms a tightening enforcement posture: audit consent flows and retention schedules now rather than at the next scheduled DPIA cycle.

Source: Data Protection Commission (Ireland)

EU 17 September 2026

Commission proposes EU KIDS Act: AI chatbots and companions must be off by default for minors

Alongside the expected minimum age of 15 for social media accounts, the proposal reaches further into AI product design: chatbots and AI companions must ship off by default for under-18 users and are barred from features that foster emotional dependency, and very large platforms lose profiling-based recommender feeds, infinite scroll and other addictive-design patterns for that age group. Enforcement follows the DSA playbook — mandatory compliance plans, independent audits, and fines up to 6% of global annual turnover. For any client building or deploying a consumer-facing chatbot or companion product with an EU user base that includes minors, this is a new, freestanding compliance layer on top of the AI Act and DSA; it is still a Commission proposal moving through Parliament and Council, not yet binding law, but the design requirement (default-off, no dependency-fostering UX) should be scoped into product roadmaps now.

Source: European Commission
