Frequently asked

Where does the EU AI Act apply to my organisation?

The EU AI Act applies by reference to where an AI system’s output is used, not where the provider or deployer is established. An organisation in the United Kingdom, the United States or any third country is caught where its AI output reaches users in the Union. Should I sign the EU Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content?

Signing is voluntary but creates a presumption of conformity with Article 50(2) and 50(4), shifting the evidentiary burden onto regulators. The initial signatory list closes 22 July 2026, 18:00 CEST — though the Code stays open for signature afterwards. We offer a written 48-hour Signatory Decision Memo to support that call.

When do Article 50 transparency obligations take effect?

Chatbot disclosure (50(1)) and deepfake/audio labelling (50(3)/(4)) apply from 2 August 2026. Machine-readable marking (50(2)) applies from 2 August 2026 for new systems, with a grandfathering window to 2 December 2026 for generative AI already on the market.
