Contact: abdulwahab@adesanyaaiadvisory.com · Dublin, Ireland (full postal address available on request).

2. What data we collect

  • Information you give us — when you use our contact form, book a consultation, or complete the EU AI Act self-assessment: your name, email, organisation, and the content of your message or responses.
  • Analytics data — if you accept analytics when prompted by our cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) to collect aggregated information about how pages are used (pages visited, approximate region, browser type). No analytics data is collected unless you explicitly accept. You can withdraw consent at any time by clicking "Decline" in the cookie banner (clearing your browser's localStorage entry ga_consent will reset the choice).
  • Cookies — this website sets no cookies itself. Google Analytics, if you accept it, will set first-party measurement cookies (_ga, _ga_*) in your browser. These are analytics cookies, not essential cookies. No cookies are set if you decline or ignore the banner.

3. Why we process it and our legal basis

  • To send updates you request (e.g. the AI Law Radar) — legal basis: consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

We do not sell your personal data. Where data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards (adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses).
