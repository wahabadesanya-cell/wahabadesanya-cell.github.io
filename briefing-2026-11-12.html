EU 11 November 2026

Digital Fairness Act proposed: curbs on profiling-based pricing and ad targeting

On 11 November the Commission adopted its proposal for a Digital Fairness Act, COM(2026) 1100, a Regulation that would replace the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive. For AI deployers the live provisions are Article 12 (limits on ad targeting based on profiling of minors or on special-category data), Article 13 (limits on profiling-based personalised pricing aimed at minors and vulnerable consumers), Article 25 (a right to reach a human after the contract is concluded) and Articles 26–27 (rules for consumers' own automated contracting systems); the explanatory memorandum presents it as complementing the AI Act. It is a proposal, not law, and now goes to Parliament and Council. Any business running algorithmic pricing, profile-based ad targeting or chatbot-only customer service should map those flows against Articles 12, 13 and 25 now, while the text can still be influenced.

Source: European Commission — Proposal for a Digital Fairness Act, COM(2026) 1100 final

Cases 6 November 2026

Kahn v Anthropic: claims over Claude Max usage-limit marketing survive dismissal

On 6 November Magistrate Judge Alex Tse (N.D. Cal., No. 3:26-cv-05763) allowed negligent misrepresentation and breach of contract claims to proceed against Anthropic over how it marketed the usage allowances of its Claude Max tiers, according to The Next Web and Glitchwire, both relying on Courthouse News; the order itself was not reviewed. The California unfair-competition and false-advertising claims were reportedly dismissed, one consumer-law claim was dismissed with leave to amend, and the claim that tightening limits mid-billing-period was unfair failed. The practical point for anyone selling an AI service: capacity multipliers in marketing are being treated as factual representations that can ground contract claims. State the baseline and the limits in the same place you make the claim.

Source: The Next Web (reporting Courthouse News); order not reviewed
